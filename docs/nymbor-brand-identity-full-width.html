Questions

Straight answers.

Is $1,500 the final price?

It's the starting price. You get your exact quote after a quick call, before any work starts.

Which AI does it work with?

Claude or ChatGPT. Each is better at different things, so we'll recommend the right one on the call based on how you work.

What if I don't use AI at all?

Then you still get a complete brand. Your files and guidelines work on their own.

Does this replace a designer?

No. It gives you strong first drafts for the everyday stuff. Launches, packaging and your website still need a designer. That's where we come in.

What happens after handoff?

You get 5 days of help to set things up and fix anything in what we delivered. New designs after that are quoted separately.

Can you refresh my existing brand?

Yes. We keep what's working, fix what isn't, and set up your AI from the result.
